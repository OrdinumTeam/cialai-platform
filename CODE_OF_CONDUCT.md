# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, respectful community regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, respect differing experiences and
take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
discriminatory language and publishing private information without permission are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards. Responses
should be proportionate: correction, warning, temporary restriction or permanent
exclusion for serious or repeated harm. Affected people should have an opportunity
to provide context privately. Reports and reporter identities must be handled discreetly.

These standards apply in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
Contact a repository maintainer through a private contact channel listed on their
GitHub profile. A dedicated conduct contact must be confirmed before public launch.

Inspired by the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
